Milan Heat Exposure

2,931 bus stops, scored for sun exposure during the hottest documented hours of Milan's hottest summer since 1951 — June 28, 2026, 12:00–18:00. Exposure score: 0 in building shadow, 0.5 in direct sun with a shelter present (a roof cuts the sun but doesn't cool like real shade), 1 in full sun with no shelter. The main view below crosses this score with how long riders actually wait.
